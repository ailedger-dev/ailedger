aiLedger starts with an open-source, append-only, verifiable record. Evidence, decisions and discoveries remain traceable—so people and AI can build on what came before.

Alongside it, aiDNA is an open context standard: a way to carry understanding between efforts. The record is the foundation. What we do with it is the frontier.
