Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

Account and Contact Information. Account email address.

Payment Information. Billing information (processed by Stripe - we do not store card data).

Internet or Network Activity Information. AI inference metadata: model name, provider, latency, status code, timestamp, and SHA-256 hashes of inputs/outputs.

2. Information we do not collect

Raw AI inputs and/or outputs are never stored. We cannot reconstruct what was sent to and/or received from any AI provider.

3. How we collect information

We obtain the personal information described above from the following sources:

  • Directly from you, such as when you create an account or provide billing information.

  • Automatically through our Services, such as AI inference metadata collected during your use of our software.

  • From third-party service providers, such as Stripe for payment processing.

4. How we use your information

We use the personal information we collect for the following business and commercial purposes:

  • To provide you with our Services and software functionality.

  • To fulfill and manage subscriptions, purchases, orders, and payments.

  • To process transactions and maintain billing records.

  • To improve our Services, including analyzing usage patterns and system performance.

  • To maintain and operate our systems and infrastructure.

  • To comply with legal obligations, including financial record-keeping requirements.

  • To enforce our Terms of Service and other agreements with you.

  • To protect the rights, property, and safety of our company, customers, and others.

5. Legal basis (GDPR)

We process your data to perform the Terms of Service agreement you have with us (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to comply with financial record-keeping obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

6. Disclosure of your information

We may disclose the personal information we collect as follows:

To Service Providers and Contractors. We disclose personal information to third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as: - Payment processors (Stripe) for billing and payment processing. - Cloud infrastructure providers (AWS via Supabase) for data storage and hosting.

These service providers are contractually obligated to keep personal information confidential and use it only for the purposes for which we disclose it to them.

For Legal Purposes. We may disclose your personal information: - To comply with any court order, law, or legal process, including responding to government or regulatory requests. - To enforce our Terms of Service and other agreements. - If we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our company, customers, or others.

Business Transfers. We may disclose personal information to a buyer or other successor in the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets.

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

7. Data residency

All data is stored in the EU, namely AWS eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, Germany) via Supabase. This applies to all plans.

8. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as otherwise legally permitted or required.

Inference logs are append-only and retained indefinitely by design - this is core to the compliance value of the service. Account data is deleted upon request, subject to our legal obligations and legitimate business needs.

We use commercially reasonable administrative, physical, and technical measures designed to protect your personal information from accidental loss or destruction and from unauthorized access, use, alteration, and disclosure. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information. Any transmission of personal information is at your own risk.

The safety and security of your information also depends on you. You are responsible for taking steps to protect your personal information against unauthorized use, disclosure, and access.
